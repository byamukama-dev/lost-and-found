How CampusFind works

From lost to found, in three simple steps.

CampusFind is a free, community-powered lost-and-found board for university students and staff. Post a report, let the board do the matching, then arrange a safe handover on campus.

Who does what

A shared responsibility

Everyone plays a part in making the board trustworthy.

Students

Report items, claim what is yours, verify ownership and meet in public campus spaces for handovers.

Finders & staff

Hand valuable items to the Campus Safety Office or a department office. Review claims fairly and keep custody details up to date.

Moderators

Administrators can remove inappropriate listings, restore mistaken removals, oversee claims and adjust member roles.

Safety first

Recover things the safe way

Most campus handovers are simple and friendly. A few sensible habits keep it that way for everyone. CampusFind never asks for passwords, PINs or payment to return an item.

Report suspicious listings or requests for money to the Campus Safety Office straight away.

Post an item safely
🏛️

Meet in public

Choose busy places like the library foyer, a café or the security office — not car parks or private rooms.

🪪

Verify ownership

Ask for a detail that is not in the public listing. Bring your student ID to collect valuable items.

🔒

Keep secrets private

Never post passwords, PINs, bank details or full ID numbers. Contact details are only shown to signed-in members.

💸

No payment needed

Returning an item should never cost money. Treat any request for a “release fee” as a scam.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered

Is CampusFind free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, reporting items and claiming found items are all free for the campus community. There are no premium tiers or fees.

Who can see my contact details?

Contact information is hidden from the public board. Lost-item contact details are available to signed-in members. For found items, only the reporter, administrators and the approved claimant can see the contact details.

How does matching work?

When you open a listing, CampusFind looks at open reports of the opposite type (lost vs found) and scores them by shared category, keywords, location and how close the dates are. You also get a notification when a new report might match one of yours.

What if nobody claims the item I found?

For valuable items, hand them to the Campus Safety Office and note that in the listing's custody field. Keep the listing open so the owner can find it. After you approve their claim, the owner confirms physical receipt on the item page. The record then stays on the board for seven days.

Can I delete or edit a report?

You can edit or delete reports you posted. Administrators can also manage reports. After confirmed receipt, the record and photo are protected for seven days. Records with unresolved ownership disputes stay protected until an administrator reviews them. Admin corrections during this period retain an audit history.

Something looks wrong on the board. What should I do?

For an incorrect handover, open the item during the seven-day review window and select “Report incorrect handover”. An administrator can review the evidence and reopen the report. Contact campus security for urgent concerns.
